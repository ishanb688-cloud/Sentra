How Sentra decides

Scam checkers usually look at one thing. Scams use several at once, so Sentra connects them before it gives a verdict.

  1. 1Read

    Separate readers study the message, every link, any payment QR address, and the app file. Nothing is opened or run.

  2. 2Weigh

    Each warning sign gets a weight, based on how strongly it shows up and how reliable that sign usually is.

  3. 3Connect

    Scams follow scripts. When signs line up, such as posing as a bank, adding pressure, then asking for a code, confidence rises.

  4. 4Explain

    You get a risk level, the exact evidence, and how the scam would play out. Sentra never says something is safe.

Sentra gives guidance based on warning signs. It cannot guarantee that anything is safe. When in doubt, contact the sender through an official number you already trust.
